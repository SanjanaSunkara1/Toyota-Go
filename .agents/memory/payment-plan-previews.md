---
name: Payment-plan previews
description: Why payment options remain selectable even when they fail the customer's financial or mileage limits.
---

Let customers select every payment plan for comparison, including an option outside their current limits. Preserve their explicit preview selection during rendering, explain the failed limits, and offer a route to review filters.

**Why:** The user reported Lease and Pay cash as broken because affordability and lease-mileage checks made the cards unclickable. Disabling comparison prevented them from discovering why those choices did not fit.

**How to apply:** Separate preview selection from eligibility. Do not weaken financial filters, silently increase savings, or start the driving scenario with an ineligible plan. Keep a defensive eligibility check at the driving entry point as well as the disabled launch action. A new vehicle or changed financial assumptions may require choosing an eligible plan again.
